## Must have
- {{city}} or within ~5 km; long-term rental (not daily/short-term)
- Not a garsónka/studio, i.e. not a single room with the kitchen in it.
  A 1-izbový byt with a separate kitchen is fine (as are 1,5-izbový, 2-izbový and larger).
  If the only room is combined with the kitchen ("kuchyňa spojená s obývacou časťou/izbou", "kuchynský kút v izbe"),
  it is a garsónka even when called 1-izbový. (In a 2-izbový a kitchen joined with the living room is fine.)
  If it's unclear whether the kitchen is separate — that's `maybe`, not `no`.
- Rent up to €750 (with or without utilities — doesn't matter)
